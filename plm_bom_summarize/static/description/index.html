Overview

Twenty identical screws become one line with quantity twenty, at the moment the CAD uploads.

This module summarises the bill of material when the CAD client uploads it: components that appear several times in the assembly are merged into a single line carrying the total quantity.

A CAD assembly lists every instance: the same screw placed sixteen times is sixteen occurrences, each in its own position. That is exactly right for the model and exactly wrong for a purchase order, and the difference is usually resolved by hand, once per structure, by somebody who has better things to do.

What it does

Merged when it arrives

The summary is applied during the upload from the CAD client, so the structure enters the system already in the form the rest of the company needs. Nothing is corrected afterwards, which means nothing is forgotten.

Quantities added, not lost

Repeated instances are not discarded: their quantities are summed onto the single surviving line, so the totals that purchasing and production work from stay right.

Positions that matter are kept

Cut components carry their position into the merge key, so items that are genuinely distinct because of where they are cut do not collapse into one another.

Nothing else changes

The module only sets the behaviour of the upload. The engineering structure keeps being what the CAD says, and every other function of the PLM works exactly as before.

  • ▪  Repeated components merged on upload from the CAD
  • ▪  Quantities summed onto the surviving line
  • ▪  Cut parts distinguished by their position
  • ▪  No manual clean up of the structure afterwards
