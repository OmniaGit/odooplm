Overview

Your supplier downloads the drawings of the order, at the revision in force today.

This module lets a supplier download, from the Odoo portal, the engineering documents of the items on their purchase order: one archive, containing the drawing of every line, generated at the moment of the download.

Sending drawings to suppliers is a job somebody does by hand, order by order: open the parts, export the sheets, attach eight files to an email, hope they are the current revision. Then the supplier calls because one is missing, or worse does not call and makes the part from the sheet you sent last year.

What it does

The supplier serves himself

From the purchase order in the portal, the whole set of documents is one download. Nobody in the office has to prepare anything, and the request that used to arrive by telephone does not arrive at all.

Generated at the moment, from current data

The documents are produced when the download is requested, from the latest revision of each item. What the supplier receives is what is in force now, not a copy that was correct when somebody attached it to an email.

One archive per order

Everything arrives as a single archive named after the order, with one document per item, so the supplier has the drawings of that order together and named for what they are.

Access is the portal's own

The download is allowed to the owner of the order, or to whoever holds a valid share link, using the standard portal access checks. There is no back door that returns documents to whoever guesses an order number.

  • ▪  Drawings of a purchase order downloaded from the portal
  • ▪  Always the current revision, produced on request
  • ▪  A single archive per order, one document per line
  • ▪  Standard portal access rules, share links included
