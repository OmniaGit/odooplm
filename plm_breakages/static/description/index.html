Breakage Management

Overview

What broke, on which part, and how often — recorded where the part lives.

This module lets you handle breakages on products and on bill of material manufacturing orders.

Breakages are known to everyone and written down by nobody: the operator who bends the same bracket every third assembly tells the shift leader, who mentions it to the technical office, which is busy. A year later the same bracket is still in the drawing, and the only trace of the problem is in the scrap figures.

What it does

A place to record them

A dedicated menu collects the breakages, with a graphical view of the products involved. A new breakage is created and filled in from there, so the record exists in the system rather than in an email.

Visible from the structure

On the bill of material a button shows which of its elements have breakages, so a structure declares its own weak points instead of hiding them one level down.

Visible in production

On the manufacturing order a button tells you whether breakages are involved, at the moment the order is being prepared and something can still be done about it.

Analysed, not just listed

An analysis report reads the accumulated records, which is what turns a series of episodes into an argument for changing the part.
