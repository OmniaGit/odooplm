Overview

Your customers turn the real model on the product page, not a photograph of it.

This module puts the engineering 3D model on the e-commerce product page, as an interactive preview the visitor can rotate, instead of a set of still pictures.

A photograph shows one side of a part. A customer choosing a component wants to see the other one: where the holes are, how the flange is oriented, whether the connector faces up. Companies answer that with more photographs, then with a downloadable STEP file, then with an email exchange that ends in a phone call.

What it does

The model already in the PLM

The preview is the document the technical office produced, not a copy prepared for the website. Nothing is exported, converted or uploaded a second time, so what the shop shows cannot drift from what engineering holds.

Published on purpose

Only documents explicitly marked as public and viewable in 3D can be attached to a product page. A model reaches the internet because somebody decided it should, not because it happened to be linked to the article.

It sits among the product images

The 3D preview is added like any other image of the product, so the shop keeps its usual gallery and the model takes its place in it, with a preview picture generated from the document itself.

No plugin on the customer side

The model is shown by the browser 3D viewer of OdooPLM: a visitor needs no CAD, no licence and no download to turn the part and look at it from the side that matters.
