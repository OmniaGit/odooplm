Overview

On a quotation, only the revisions you are willing to commit to.

This module restricts the product selection on the sale order lines to the products that are at their latest engineering revision, or that carry no engineering code at all.

A salesman preparing a quotation sees the whole history of a part number and has no way to tell which of those lines is the product the company actually builds today. Quoting a superseded revision turns into an order for something the workshop no longer makes.

What it does

The catalogue answers with today's product

On the lines of a quotation or an order the product search returns the current revisions only, so what is offered to a customer is what the company is able to deliver.

Only where it belongs

The restriction is applied on the document lines where it matters, and nowhere else. Searching the product catalogue from anywhere else in Odoo keeps returning everything, because a warehouse operation or an inventory count has every right to see an old revision.

What counts as selectable, decided by you

Released parts are always selectable. Which other engineering states join them is a system parameter, so a company that also sells from the confirmed state can say so instead of patching the module.

Non engineering products untouched

Products without an engineering code — consumables, services, everything the technical office never drew — are never filtered out.

  • ▪  Current revisions only, on the sale order lines
  • ▪  Released revisions selectable, plus the states you configure
  • ▪  Products without an engineering code are never filtered
  • ▪  Applied on the document lines, not on the whole catalogue

Part of OdooPLM, the Product Lifecycle Management suite by OmniaSolutions. The additional selectable states are configured through the sales_only_latest_params system parameter. Released under AGPL-3.
