Overview

Change requests that travel as work, not as messages.

This module improves the communication process on OdooPLM. It lets you manage modification requests coming from Odoo and from the CAD application, manage the engineering request and the engineering order, and control the status of your requests against the bill of material.

A change starts as a sentence: this part interferes, this tolerance is wrong, the customer wants it shorter. What happens next decides whether it becomes a controlled modification or a conversation that ends in somebody's memory.

What it does

Requests from both sides

A modification request can be raised from Odoo or from the CAD application, and lands in the same place either way. The designer at the workstation and the person in the office are working on one list.

Request, then order

The engineering request and the engineering order are distinct steps, so evaluating a change and authorising it are two decisions rather than one, and the second one leaves a record.

A state of its own

Activities carry a PLM state of their own, moved by explicit actions: in progress, done, exception, cancelled, or escalated into an engineering change order. What was refused is as visible as what was approved.

Checked against the structure

The status of the requests is controlled against the bill of material, so a change is read in terms of the parts it touches rather than as an isolated ticket.
