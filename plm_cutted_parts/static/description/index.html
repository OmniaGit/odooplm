Cutted Parts

Overview

Raw material that becomes a part, handled in the structure instead of in someone's head.

This module improves the capability of your OdooPLM for managing the cutted parts.

A shaft is a bar cut to length, a plate is a sheet the laser cut out. On the drawing there is one part; in the warehouse there is raw material and an operation. Where that distinction is not in the structure it lives in the memory of whoever prepares the orders.

What it does

From the part, in one action

From Search on Engineering Parts select a product and run Create Normal Bom from the action menu. The treatment of the raw material is chosen there, when the structure is created.

None

No change is performed on the bill of material: the part stays as it is.

Explode

The explode option creates an extra level of bill of material, so the raw material appears as its own step under the finished part.

Replace

The replace option replaces the product that owns the raw material with the raw material itself.

And a report to check it

A report on the bill of material lists all the cutted parts related to it, so what was exploded or replaced can be read at a glance instead of being inferred.

The action menu on the engineering parts, where the structure is created.

The choice that decides how raw material is treated: none, explode or replace.

Exploded: the raw material becomes an extra level of the structure.

Replaced: the part that owns the raw material gives way to the material itself.

  • ▪  Raw material handled when the normal BOM is created
  • ▪  Three treatments: none, explode, replace
  • ▪  An extra BOM level where the cut deserves one
  • ▪  A report listing the cutted parts of a structure
