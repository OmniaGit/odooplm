Overview

On a purchase order, only the revisions a supplier should be making.

This module restricts the product selection on the purchase order lines to the products that are at their latest engineering revision, or that carry no engineering code at all.

A buyer types the beginning of a part number and gets six lines back: revision 0, revision 1, revision 2, the same part four times over. Picking the wrong one costs a delivery of pieces that no longer fit, and the mistake surfaces weeks later, at the goods entrance.

What it does

The list is already right

On the lines of a purchase order the product search returns the current revisions only. The buyer does not have to know which revision is in force: the choice is made for him, at the moment he types.

Only where it belongs

The restriction is applied on the document lines where it matters, and nowhere else. Searching the product catalogue from anywhere else in Odoo keeps returning everything, because a warehouse operation or an inventory count has every right to see an old revision.

What counts as selectable, decided by you

Released parts are always selectable. Which other engineering states join them is a system parameter, so a company that also sells from the confirmed state can say so instead of patching the module.

Non engineering products untouched

Products without an engineering code — consumables, services, everything the technical office never drew — are never filtered out.

  • ▪  Current revisions only, on the purchase order lines
  • ▪  Released revisions selectable, plus the states you configure
  • ▪  Products without an engineering code are never filtered
  • ▪  Applied on the document lines, not on the whole catalogue

Part of OdooPLM, the Product Lifecycle Management suite by OmniaSolutions. The additional selectable states are configured through the purchase_only_latest_params system parameter. Released under AGPL-3.
