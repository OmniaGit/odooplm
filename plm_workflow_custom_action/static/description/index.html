Workflow Custom Actions

Overview

Your rules attached to the engineering workflow, without writing a module.

Automate your workflow actions with the standard Odoo automated actions.

Every company has its own habits around a release. Notify purchasing when a part is released, refuse to release a part whose drawing is still checked out, write the code into another field, tell the ERP of the plant next door. These rules are real, they differ from company to company, and they usually end up either in somebody's memory or in a custom module that nobody wants to maintain.

What it does

Bound to the transition

An action is declared between two states of the engineering workflow: from draft to confirmed, from confirmed to released, and so on. It fires on the passage, which is the moment the rule is about.

Before or after, on purpose

You choose whether it runs before or after the transition. Before is where you check and can stop it; after is where you tell the rest of the company what happened.

On parts or on documents

The same mechanism applies to products and to engineering documents, because the rules that guard a drawing are rarely the same ones that guard a part.

Narrowed to what it concerns

A domain limits the action to the records it is meant for, so a rule for castings does not fire on screws, and one plant's habits do not become everybody's.

Built from server actions

What the action does is a chain of standard Odoo server actions. No new language to learn, and anything the automation framework can already do is available here as well.
