Overview

The engineering documents of the part, one tap away on the shop floor tablet.

This module makes the PLM documents available inside the work order worksheet of the Odoo shop floor application.

The operator has a tablet with the work instructions and, somewhere else entirely, the drawing: printed on a sheet that is two revisions old, or on a folder nobody opens with oily hands. The half of the information that is under revision control is the half that does not reach the bench — and the 3D model, the one thing that answers most questions at the bench, never gets there at all.

What it does

The documents come to the order

From the work order the engineering documents of the product are opened directly, without leaving the shop floor application and without searching the archive.

Only what production should see

Only the documents marked as production documents are shown, so the operator gets the drawing and the instructions rather than the whole engineering history of the part.

The 3D model, not only the drawing

With plm_web_3d installed, the same documents open in the browser 3D viewer: the operator turns the model on the tablet, looks at the part from the side that matters and puts it down again. No CAD, no licence, no file to download.

The workshop answers back

The same viewer carries the markup tools. The operator circles what does not fit, draws an arrow, writes a line and submits it: the remark travels back to the engineering document as a message, with the picture attached. The bench stops being the end of the line and becomes the last reviewer.

Read only, on purpose

The documents are opened in a view that cannot create or delete anything. The shop floor consults, it does not edit, and the archive cannot be damaged from the bench.

At the current revision

What is shown is the document held by the PLM, so the drawing on the tablet follows the revision instead of following whoever last used the printer.

The shop floor application: from the work order to the engineering documents of the part, and back.

The 3D viewer with the markup tools: circle, arrow, text and a message. What the operator marks here reaches the people who drew the part.
