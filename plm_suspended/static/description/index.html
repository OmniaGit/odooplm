Suspended State

Overview

Take a part out of circulation without declaring it dead.

This module adds the suspended state to the engineering workflow, for products and for documents.

Not every stop is final. The supplier of a casting has a quality problem, a batch is under investigation, a design decision is waiting for a customer answer. The part must not be used in the meantime, but obsoleting it would be a lie: it is not superseded, it is on hold, and in a few weeks it will very likely go back to work.

What it does

A state for the pause

Suspended sits in the engineering workflow next to the states you already use, so a part on hold is visibly on hold rather than quietly left in draft or wrongly declared obsolete.

It remembers where it came from

The state the item held before being suspended is kept. Lifting the suspension puts it back exactly there, so a released part returns released and a confirmed one returns confirmed, without anybody having to remember.

Parts and documents alike

Products and engineering documents can both be suspended, because a drawing under discussion has to stop circulating just as much as the part it describes.

Suspend and restore as actions

Both the suspension and its lifting are explicit actions on the record, which means they leave a trace and can be driven by the automated actions you already use.
