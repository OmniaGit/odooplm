Web Revision

Overview

A new revision without opening the CAD.

A revision is the moment a company either keeps control of its product or loses it. Everything hangs off it: the drawing the supplier is quoting, the structure production is building, the spare parts a customer will order in five years. And in most companies only the few people with a CAD licence installed can start one, so every request from quality, sales or after sales queues up on the technical office.

This module moves that action to the browser. An authorised user creates the new revision from Odoo, chooses what has to travel with it, and the whole set moves together instead of drifting apart.

What it does

Revise from the browser

The new revision of a product or of a document is created from Odoo, with no CAD " "session involved. The previous revision is preserved, not replaced: history stays readable, and what was released to a supplier remains what it was.

You choose what travels

Four choices, made when you revise: the linked document, the engineering bill of material, the manufacturing one, the spare one. Take all of them for a design change, only the document for a drawing correction.

Rules that protect the data

Only what has been released can be revised, and the module says so instead of failing quietly. A draft is still work in progress, and revising it would create a version of something that never existed.

Documents follow their product

The documents attached to the old revision are re-linked to the new one, so the new version is born complete rather than as an empty shell somebody has to fill again by hand.
