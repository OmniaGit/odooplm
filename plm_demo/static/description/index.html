Overview

A populated PLM to look at, instead of an empty one.

Installing the PLM gives you a working system with nothing in it. This module fills it with a complete little product, so the suite can be evaluated, demonstrated and taught on real data.

An empty database is the worst place to judge a PLM. Revision management, spare part manuals, document relations and 3D markups all look the same when there is nothing to apply them to: a set of empty list views. The questions that decide a purchase — what happens to the structures when I revise this part, where does the drawing of that component end up — cannot even be asked.

What it does

A real product, not sample rows

LSU-100, a linear shaft support unit: twelve parts over three levels of bill of material, with materials and weights taken from the solids. Small enough to follow in an afternoon, complete enough to behave like a real one.

The documents a CAD upload produces

A STEP and a 3MF for every part, dimensioned 2D sheets for the machined ones and for the two assemblies, each carrying its printable PDF, all with previews. Hung off each other the way the CAD client hangs them: the model at the top, its viewer file and its drawing below.

Spare parts, end to end

A spare bill of material for each assembly, whose rows carry the same positions that are ballooned on the assembly drawing, so the Spare Parts Manual can be printed and read as a customer would read it.

3D markups and their conversation

Two markups on the top assembly with the chatter messages that go with them, because the collaboration around a model is one of the things a demonstration has to show and a screenshot cannot.

Generated, and yours to keep

The geometry is produced with cadquery and ezdxf by OmniaSolutions and carries the same licence as the module: no third party CAD model is redistributed, and nothing has to be removed before showing it to a customer.

It removes itself

Everything is created through the ORM with explicit external ids, so uninstalling the module takes the demonstration data away again and leaves the database as it was.
